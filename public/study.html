Consent

You are invited to try Commit Doctor, a tool that summarizes a team's GitHub commits. You will answer ten short questions about recent activity in a repository: five using GitHub's own commit history and five using Commit Doctor. Then you will rate Commit Doctor on ten statements.

What is recorded: your participant code, your answers, how long each answer took, and your ratings and comments. Your name, email and GitHub account are not recorded.

How it is used: only to evaluate Commit Doctor for our project, and reported only as group results.

Your rights: taking part is voluntary. You may stop at any time, and you may ask for your answers to be deleted, under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

Keep that tab open next to this one. Each question is timed from when it appears, so take your time but don't wait between them.
